# Key findings

- **Renewables grew from 37.8% of generation in 2019 to 55.5% in 2025**; wind + solar alone went from 26.5% to 41.4%, driven mostly by solar (5.6% → 19.8%).
- **Prices peaked in 2022 at an average of 167.5 €/MWh** (gas crisis), versus 65.3 €/MWh in 2025.
- **Since 2023, days with the most wind + solar are much cheaper:** average price 109.7 €/MWh on days below 30% vs 36.4 €/MWh above 50% (159 and 195 days).
- **Controlling for demand and season**, each extra 10 percentage points of wind + solar is associated with a price change of -22.9 €/MWh (linear regression, R² = 0.60, n = 1096 days, 2023 onward).
- **Demand in 2025 was 256 TWh**, compared with 265 TWh in 2019.

Days with an average price below 10 €/MWh: 2019: 2, 2020: 5, 2021: 6, 2022: 2, 2023: 9, 2024: 48, 2025: 13

_Note: association, not proof of causation. Gas prices, hydro conditions and interconnection flows also move prices and are not in this dataset._
